## News and Highlights

* \[**23 June 2026**\]: The project is presented at the *"Fishbowl discussion on Foundation Models"* of the [**ESA  φ-week Summit**](https://philab.esa.int/phinnovation/).

* \[**07 May 2026**\]: The paper *"From Satellite Data and Geo-Tagged Field Photos to Reliable Agricultural Reference Data"* is presented at [**EGU 2026**](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-16567.html).

* \[**20 April 2026**\]: The paper *"From Satellite Data and Geo-Tagged Field Photos to Reliable Agricultural Reference Data"* is accepted for oral presentation at [**IGARSS 2026**](https://2026.ieeeigarss.org/view_paper.php?PaperNum=2942&SessionID=1476).

* \[**20 April 2026**\]: The paper [*"Combining VLM and Geo-Tagged Field Photos for Scalable Agricultural Reference Data"*](https://2026.ieeeigarss.org/view_paper.php?PaperNum=3309&SessionID=1479) is accepted for oral presentation at **IGARSS 2026**.
