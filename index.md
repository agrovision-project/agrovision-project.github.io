## News and Highlights

* \[**23 June 2026**\]: The AgroVision project is presented during the *"Fishbowl discussion on Foundation Models"* segment of the [**ESA  φ-Week Summit**](https://philab.esa.int/phinnovation/).

* \[**07 May 2026**\]: The paper *"From Satellite Data and Geo-Tagged Field Photos to Reliable Agricultural Reference Data"* is presented at [**EGU**](https://meetingorganizer.copernicus.org/EGU26/EGU26-16567.html).

* \[**20 April 2026**\]: The paper *"From Satellite Data and Geo-Tagged Field Photos to Reliable Agricultural Reference Data"* is accepted for oral presentation at [**IGARSS**](https://2026.ieeeigarss.org/view_paper.php?PaperNum=2942&SessionID=1476).

* \[**20 April 2026**\]: The paper *"Combining VLM and Geo-Tagged Field Photos for Scalable Agricultural Reference Data"* is accepted for oral presentation at [**IGARSS**](https://2026.ieeeigarss.org/view_paper.php?PaperNum=2942&SessionID=1476).
